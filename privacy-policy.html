Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026
Oyunexi: Game Hub does not sell your data and does not use advertising, analytics or tracking. All games can be played offline without sending any data. Only the optional Online mode sends a small amount of data, solely to connect you with the players in your room.

1. Information We Collect

Oyunexi: Game Hub is a mini game collection. It does not require an account, and offline play does not send any data off your device.

Online mode (optional). When you create or join an online room, the following data is sent to our online game service (Google Firebase):

  • Nickname: the name you type in the room screen (optional; if left empty, "Player" is shown). It is shown only to the players in your room.
  • Anonymous player ID: a random ID created by Firebase Authentication when you first use Online mode. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or Google account.
  • Room and game data: the 6-digit room code, the game type, seat order, game moves (for example a card played or a piece moved) and connection status times used to show whether the other player is still connected.

To provide the service, Firebase may also process technical information such as your IP address, as described in Google's privacy terms. We do not collect your email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

2. Local Game Data

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Scores and progress: best scores, levels, badges and game statistics.
  • Preferences: language, sound and vibration settings, favourite games, recently played games and menu order.
  • Online mode: your nickname and the anonymous player ID, so you keep the same identity next time.

This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers, except the online data listed in section 1 while you play online.

3. How We Use Data

Data is used solely to:

  • Show your scores, progress and settings inside the app
  • Create online rooms, let other players join with the room code and deliver game moves between the players in the same room
  • Show the players' nicknames and whether a player has disconnected

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any commercial tracking purpose, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Online mode uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication with anonymous sign-in, and Firebase Realtime Database hosted in the European Union, Belgium region) as a service provider to store room data temporarily. See Firebase privacy and security.

The app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social login or other tracking tools.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Online rooms (nicknames, seat order and game moves) expire 6 hours after they are created and are then deleted automatically. A room is also closed when its host leaves.
  • Anonymous player ID: clearing the app data or uninstalling the app removes it from your device. You can ask us to delete the ID from Firebase by writing to the contact address below.
  • Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Oyunexi: Game Hub does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app contains no in-app purchases, advertisements or chat. In Online mode the only free text is the optional nickname, which is visible only to the players who know the room code. We recommend not using a real full name as a nickname.

7. Security

All online data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database security rules allow players to change only their own seat and moves, and only the host can close a room.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
