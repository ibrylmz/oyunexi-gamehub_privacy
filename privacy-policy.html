Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: JULY 2026
Oyunexi: Game Hub does not collect, store, sell, or share personally identifiable information.

1. Information We Collect

Oyunexi: Game Hub is a mini game collection. The current version does not require users to create an account and does not ask users to provide personal information.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

2. Local Game Data

The app may store the following data locally on your device:

  • Best scores: Scores or records achieved in individual mini games.
  • Game preferences: Settings such as selected language.

This data stays on your device and is used only to improve your gameplay experience. It is not sent to our servers.

3. How We Use Data

Local game data is used solely to:

  • Show your best scores inside the app
  • Remember your selected language
  • Improve continuity between game sessions

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any commercial tracking purpose.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version of Oyunexi: Game Hub does not use third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social login, cloud leaderboards, or external tracking tools.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the app. You can remove local data by clearing the app storage from your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Oyunexi: Game Hub does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The current version contains no in-app purchases, advertisements, social features, or user-generated content.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
